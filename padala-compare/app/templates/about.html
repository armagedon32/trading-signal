About {{ site_name }}

{{ site_name }} is a free tool for Filipinos abroad. Type the amount you want to send home and it shows, side by side, how many pesos each remittance app will deliver today – fees and exchange-rate mark-ups included.

Why it exists

Most people pick one app and stick with it. But rates move every day and differ by several percent between apps. On a ₱30,000 padala that difference can be ₱500–₱1,500 – every month. This site makes the check take ten seconds.

How we make money

Some "Open" buttons are referral links. When someone signs up through them and sends money, the app pays us a small referral fee. You pay nothing extra. The ranking is computed only from the pesos received – a referral link never moves an app up the list, and apps that pay us nothing are shown the same way.

Accuracy

  • Quotes come from the providers' own public rate calculators and are refreshed every few minutes.
  • Mid-market rates and the 30-day chart come from Wise and the European Central Bank (via Frankfurter).
  • Promotional rates are marked. Cash pick-up, card pay-in and very large amounts can have different fees.
  • The amount shown inside the app at the moment you pay is always the final one.

Telegram alerts

Our free bot can message you when the dollar (or dirham, riyal, pound…) reaches a level you choose, and can send a short morning summary. We store only your Telegram chat id, currency and target rate; send /stop to delete them.

{{ site_name }} is not a money transfer service and does not handle your money. Not financial advice.
