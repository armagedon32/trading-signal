How this works

Why is one app's rate lower even with "zero fee"?

Apps earn from the exchange rate. A "no fee" transfer with a rate 2% below mid-market costs you 2% – on {{ currency.symbol }}{{ amount|money(0) }} that is real money. We only compare the final pesos your family receives.

What is the "new-customer promo"?

Remitly and some others give a better rate on your first transfer (usually capped). Great for a first send; the regular rate applies afterwards, so check back before your next padala.

Where do the numbers come from?

Straight from the providers' own public calculators, refreshed every few minutes, and the mid-market rate from Wise / the European Central Bank. Rates change during the day – the amount inside the app at the moment you pay is final.
